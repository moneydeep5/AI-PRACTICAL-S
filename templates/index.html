MODEL NOTES

How the mini AI works

MoodLens uses a lightweight multinomial-style Naive Bayes classifier built directly in Python. This keeps the demonstration easy to inspect and run locally.

01

Tokenization

The text is converted to lowercase words. Common stopwords are removed so words with stronger signal have more influence.

02

Training

A small built-in labeled dataset teaches the classifier which words are commonly associated with positive, negative, and neutral language.

03

Prediction

The model calculates log-probability scores for all three classes and converts them into an easy-to-read confidence distribution.

04

Explanation

For known words, the app compares class-specific probabilities. Words with stronger differences become the displayed evidence.

CORE IDEA

Naive Bayes scoring

score(class) = log P(class) + Σ log P(word | class)

The implementation uses Laplace smoothing so unseen words do not break the calculation.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Ethics & explainability

This prototype is designed for education. It should demonstrate AI concepts, not make high-impact decisions about real people.

01

Bias & dataset limits

The training examples are small and handcrafted. Language, slang, culture, and context can be underrepresented, so predictions may be biased or wrong.

02

Privacy

Text is processed locally by the Flask app and is not intentionally stored. In a real deployment, logs, retention, and access controls must still be designed carefully.

03

Human oversight

A confidence score is not proof. A human should review important decisions instead of treating the prediction as an objective truth.

04

Explainability

The app exposes the influential words used by the model. This improves transparency, but it does not guarantee a complete causal explanation of language.

05

Fair use

Do not use this student prototype for hiring, admissions, medical diagnosis, credit, policing, or other high-impact profiling.

06

Accuracy & testing

The demo should be evaluated with a larger test set before any real use. Precision, recall, class balance, and errors should be reported.

REPORT SUMMARY

Responsible AI statement

MoodLens AI demonstrates that an NLP system should communicate not only what it predicts, but also why, where its data came from, and where its limits are. For a college project, the most important ethical principle is to keep the scope educational and maintain human judgment for consequential decisions.
